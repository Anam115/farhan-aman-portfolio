Professional Profile

About Dr. Farhan Aman

Dedicated medical practitioner with clinical training across inpatient, emergency, and surgical environments.

Dr. Farhan Aman is an enthusiastic and diligent medical graduate who earned his Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) from Ayub Medical College, Abbottabad. He completed his rigorous one-year clinical house job at Ayub Teaching Hospital, cultivating hands-on procedural aptitude and patient-first clinical judgment across otolaryngology, pediatrics, general surgery, and internal medicine.

Recognized for his ability to handle demanding clinical situations with composure under pressure, Dr. Farhan approaches healthcare challenges with a disciplined, structured methodology. He adapts quickly to dynamic ward environments and complex clinical workflows, maintaining thorough patient documentation and active clinical communication.

In addition to patient care, Dr. Farhan is deeply committed to academic medicine, clinical audit standards, and evidence-based practice. He has conducted formal research into healthcare topics and co-authored published research evaluating the utilization of artificial intelligence among medical and dental students.
